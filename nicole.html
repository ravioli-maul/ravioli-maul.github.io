Nicole Health Coach

Nicole Health Coach is a private, personal app used by a single person, its owner, to keep a personal training and wellness log.

With the account owner's permission, it reads workout, heart rate, sleep and body measurement data from the owner's own Google Health account, and can write workout sessions back to it. The data is used only to track the owner's own training and give the owner coaching feedback.

The app is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not sell, share or advertise with any data.
