Privacy Policy

Nicole Health Coach · Last updated 9 November 2026

Who this app is for

Nicole Health Coach is a private app built for one person, its owner, and used only with the owner's own Google account. It is not available to anyone else.

What data it accesses

With the account owner's explicit consent through Google sign-in, the app accesses these Google Health data types: exercise sessions, heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, and health measurements such as weight, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate and skin temperature. It may also write exercise sessions to the owner's Google Health account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to keep the owner's personal training log and to give the owner feedback and coaching on their training, recovery and health. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and security

Data is copied into a private database on a computer operated for the owner and accessible only to the owner and the owner's personal assistant software. Access tokens are stored with restricted file permissions. No data is published or made public.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share data with third parties. It does not transfer data to anyone other than the account owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Nicole Health Coach's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted on the owner's request.
